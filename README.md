## About

I'm an Enterprise Security Architect at Booz Allen Hamilton, working in the firm's CISO organization on secure product launch and R&D assurance. That means security reviews, hardening, and making sure R&D teams are building things securely from the start (FedRAMP, CMMC, SSDF, Secure-by-Design). I also lead agentic secure-code remediation and ATO-package validation work, turning vulnerability findings into pull-request patches and using AI agent swarms for high-assurance authorization review. Before this I spent over a decade on federal CDM, working from solution architect up to large-scale engineering leadership across multiple agencies.

I'm also pursuing a PhD at George Mason in Systems Engineering & Operations Research. My research applies causal AI to predicting when large-scale cyber defense implementations are about to go sideways, built on what I've seen go wrong (and right) on CDM over the years.
